A specific claim = a concrete price or rating written in the answer (e.g. 1199 lei, 4.6/5) checked against the live database.

Trust is an integration problem,
not a model-capability problem.

  • —Best out of 8 configs is the cheapest model: Gemini Flash-Lite · composite 0.807 · ~$0.0003/query · p95 4.2s.
  • —More reasoning was worse: the big model at high reasoning was the worst (0.492) and slowest (12.1s p95).
  • —Grounding held everywhere: groundedness ≥ 0.89 across all 8 configurations.

Contributions and honest limits.

  • —Contribution: an integration architecture + an honest evaluation against documented production failures.
  • —Grounding is structural and measured.
  • —Limits: no user study · not tested against a standard product-search baseline · ~200-product seeded catalog.
  • —Next: a within-subjects user study on trust and purchase confidence · fine-tuning a smaller model
